# Lapyn

Use Claude to create and evolve UI designs in Lapyn, from localized content to implementation-ready code. Claude can create new screens, restyle existing ones, translate designs, inspect their structure, and verify visual changes before reporting back.

You don't need to keep Lapyn open. Changes are saved directly to your Lapyn project and are there when you return.

## Things to ask

- "Create a sign-up screen for my app in Lapyn."
- "Translate the onboarding screens into German and Spanish."
- "Give the settings screen a darker, calmer look."
- "Explain how the home screen is structured."

## Getting started

You need a Lapyn account. The first time Claude connects to Lapyn, you'll be asked to sign in, choose which projects it can access, and whether access should be read-only or include editing.

You can disconnect Claude at any time from your Lapyn account.

## Your data

Claude accesses Lapyn through Lapyn's servers. Information it reads from your projects may become part of your conversation with Claude.

The plugin does not run software on your computer.
